Selah Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Selah does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Selah is an iPhone app for learning Scripture, developed by Matthew Giraldo. This policy explains how Selah handles information.

Information we collect

None. Selah does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information. Selah does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To work, Selah saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • Which lessons you've completed, and your answers' scores
  • Your review schedule and how well you've remembered each word
  • The days you've used Selah, to show your streak
  • Any reflections you choose to write
  • The focus you picked during setup

This information never leaves your device through Selah. We cannot see it, and no one else can access it through the app.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, Apple may include Selah's on-device data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Those backups are managed by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Selah contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not include third-party software that collects data, and it does not connect to the internet.

Deleting your data

You're always in control of what Selah keeps:

  • To delete a reflection, open Profile › Reflections and swipe left on it.
  • To erase all progress and reflections, open Profile › Reset progress.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Selah does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If Selah ever changes how it handles information, for example by adding optional syncing between devices, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that change takes effect.
