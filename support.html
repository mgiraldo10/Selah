Selah Support

Help with Selah, the iPhone app for learning Scripture one word a day.

Questions, problems, or suggestions? Email mgiraldo10@icloud.com and you'll hear back personally.

Common questions

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing bad. Your streak stays intact through the end of the next day, and when you come back, Selah picks up right where you left off. There are no penalties, hearts, or lives.

How does review work?

After you learn a word, Selah brings it back for a short review the next day, then at growing intervals: two days, four days, a week, and so on. If you miss a review question, the word comes back tomorrow. Words that are due appear on the Today screen and at the end of your next lesson.

Where are my reflections stored?

Only on your iPhone. Selah has no accounts and never sends anything off your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one. Selah's data is included in standard iPhone backups.

How do I delete a reflection or start over?

To delete one reflection, open Profile › Reflections and swipe left on it. To erase everything, open Profile › Reset progress.

How do I see the introduction again?

Open Profile › Replay the introduction.

Which Bible translation does Selah use?

All Scripture in Selah is quoted from the King James Version.

Does Selah need an internet connection?

No. Everything is built into the app, so it works anywhere.

I found a mistake in a lesson.

Thank you. Accuracy matters a great deal here. Please email the word, the screen you saw it on, and what looks wrong.
